Understanding Self-Balancing Binary Search Trees

⚖️
Real-World Analogy: Balancing a Scale (天平平衡)
Just like a scale needs equal weight on both sides to stay balanced, an AVL tree maintains balance by ensuring the height difference between left and right subtrees is at most 1. When it becomes unbalanced, we perform rotations—like adjusting weights—to restore equilibrium! ⚖️✨
